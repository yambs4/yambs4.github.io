About

Economics with a practical edge.

I combine research design, field experience, and data science to make complex development questions answerable.

I am an Economist (Ph.D.) and data scientist based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. My work spans impact evaluation, monitoring and evaluation, economic policy research, teaching, and applied data systems.

I currently work as a freelance consultant and assistant professor. I have also served as a college dean and research and community services coordinator, leading teams, supervising graduate research, and working with national and international partners.
